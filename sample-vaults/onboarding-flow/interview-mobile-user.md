Freelance designer, signed up on iPhone. Got through the full onboarding but said it felt "surprisingly long." Main quote: "I kept thinking it was almost done and then there was another screen."

Specific friction points:
- Step 6 asks for billing info even on the free trial — felt jarring and unexpected
- The progress bar shows steps but not time, so each step feels potentially huge
- Back button on step 3 cleared her inputs, had to re-enter

One positive: she liked that she could skip the "invite teammates" step. Said if that had been required she would have dropped off.

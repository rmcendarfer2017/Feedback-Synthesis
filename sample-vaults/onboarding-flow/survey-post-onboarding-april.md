Post-onboarding survey results (n=47, sent to all users who completed onboarding in April)

Q: How long did onboarding take?
- Under 5 min: 8%
- 5-10 min: 31%
- 10-20 min: 44%
- Over 20 min: 17%

Q: Did onboarding meet your time expectations?
- Yes: 38%
- No, took longer than expected: 55%
- No, took shorter than expected: 7%

Q: What was the most confusing step?
- Role selection: 34%
- Billing/plan selection: 28%
- Team invite: 18%
- Profile setup: 12%
- Other: 8%

Q: How confident do you feel using the product after onboarding?
- Very confident: 19%
- Somewhat confident: 43%
- Neutral: 24%
- Not very confident: 14%
